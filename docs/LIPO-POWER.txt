EXTERNAL USB-C LiPo POWER FOR THE COMPACT S3-ZERO CARRIER

This is an external power accessory for counter.kicad_pcb. The PCB retains
the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero and takes power through its USB socket.
It has no charger or boost converter of its own; J1 VBUS is unconnected.

PARTS SELECTED IN THE SHOPPING LIST
1. EEMB LP103454 protected 3.7V 2000mAh LiPo, JST 2.0mm, one battery:
   https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08214DJLJ
2. HiLetgo TP4056 Type-C charger with protection, use one board:
   https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PKND8KG
3. Newzoll fixed 5V / 500mA USB boost module, use one board:
   https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MQG3T7V
4. elechawk JST-PH 2.0mm pigtail pairs, use for removable connections:
   https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07NWD5NTN
5. USB-A to USB-C cable for the S3-Zero; USB-A to USB-C charging cable
   and a regulated 5V USB wall supply rated at least 1A.

CONNECTIONS (assemble with battery and USB disconnected)
- Battery positive -> TP4056 B+; battery negative -> TP4056 B-.
- TP4056 OUT+ -> removable connector -> boost IN+.
- TP4056 OUT- -> same removable connector -> boost IN-.
- Boost USB-A output -> USB cable -> ESP32 DevKit USB input.
- Use the protected OUT pads for the boost input, not the battery B pads.
- Do not connect raw battery voltage to 3V3, VIN or the raw display.
- Confirm polarity with a meter; compatible JST plugs can have opposite
  red/black wire placement. Match electrical + and -, not wire colors alone.
- Insulate all solder joints and the backs of both modules; keep the battery
  clear of PCB pins and sharp edges. No module mounting footprint is assumed.

CHARGING
Unplug the removable connection between the TP4056 and boost board BEFORE
connecting USB-C power to the charger. Leave the battery connected to B+/B-.
The TP4056 module does not provide load sharing. The counter must be off and
the boost board disconnected from OUT while charging. After charging, remove
the charging USB cable and reconnect the boost board for play.
When programming, unplug the DevKit from the boost USB output and connect
its USB socket to the computer instead. No firmware changes are needed.

VERIFICATION STATUS
The battery listing specifies 4.2V charging and up to 2A maximum charge
current, compatible with the selected charger's 1A nominal maximum. The
boost's published output rating is 5V/500mA. This is a specification-based
selection for the current counter, without added motors or other loads.
No physical fit, battery runtime, temperature or startup testing has been
performed. Check the boost output voltage before plugging in the ESP32 and
test one assembled prototype before building a batch.
